The short version

This website doesn't ask for, store or collect your personal information itself. There are no accounts, no forms, no newsletter and no analytics, and the site sets no cookies of its own. When you search for a constituency, the lookup happens entirely in your browser — what you type is never sent to me or saved.

That said, a few third parties are involved in delivering this site and the advertising on it, and they process some data. This page explains who, and what your choices are.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who runs this site

Stop The Split is an independent project run by an individual. For any privacy question or request, contact hello.stopreform@protonmail.com.

What this site does not collect

  • No names, email addresses or other details are requested on the site.
  • Constituency searches are processed in your browser and are not transmitted or stored.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are run by me, and I set no cookies of my own.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, a US company, so this data may be processed in the United States or other countries outside the UK. Like any web host, Netlify receives technical information when your browser requests a page — such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested — and keeps this in server logs for a limited period for security and operational purposes. See Netlify's privacy policy. I don't have access to your identity from this data.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

In the UK and European Economic Area, ads that use cookies are only personalised with your consent. You'll be asked for that choice through a consent message, and you can change or withdraw it at any time through the privacy settings offered on this site.

You can also opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • aboutads.info/choices
  • youronlinechoices.com/uk

To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you email me

If you contact me (for example to send a correction, or about the Support page), I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and will not share it or add it to any mailing list. I keep correspondence only as long as it's useful to do so. Email is provided by Proton; see Proton's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to sources such as YouGov, PollCheck, Electoral Calculus and the UK Parliament. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which I don't control.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data held about you, including access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Because this site doesn't collect personal data itself, most of these will be exercised with Google or Netlify — but you're welcome to contact me at the address above about anything. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If how this site handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
